# Pandora logo

Created with the built-in image generation tool using Pandora's photograph as the reference. The final PNG is the simplified sleeping-cat symbol, with cream, taupe, warm brown, and dusty pink colors.

## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: primary standalone logo symbol for the Pandora mobile productivity app.
Input image: reference photograph of Pandora, the real lynx point cat. Use only the sleeping cat as inspiration, ignore all screenshot UI, furniture, bedding, video overlay and surroundings.
Primary request: create a refined, distinctive minimalist logo based on this specific cat's curled sleeping pose. Her cream body forms a soft circular crescent, her small tucked head sits near the upper right inside the curve, her tapered tail curves inward along the lower arc. Keep the recognizable pointed ears, gently closed eye, subtle lynx-point markings, and one small dusty-pink inner-ear accent. The silhouette should feel like one coherent compact mark, peaceful and welcoming.
Style: crisp flat vector-style brand design rendered as a high resolution PNG, simple rounded geometric curves, restrained broad shapes and clean negative space, very few internal details. Professional app identity, legible at small icon sizes.
Palette: warm ivory fur, muted taupe point markings, dark warm mushroom-brown structural contours for contrast, tiny dusty rose ear accent, based on the photographed cat.
Composition: one centered symbol on a square canvas with generous clear margin, no typography, no wordmark, no layout sheet, no additional variants, no icon tile behind it.
Background: genuinely transparent, preserve alpha.
Avoid: realism, fur texture, gradients, shadows, 3D, intricate thin lines, generic front-facing cat head, exaggerated cartoon face, accessories, stars, lettering, watermark.

## Final refinement prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Refine the supplied cat logo into a much simpler professional app logo. Preserve its curled sleeping pose, circular silhouette, tucked head at upper right, pointed ears, closed eye, cream/taupe/brown palette and tiny dusty pink accents. Replace all fur texture, shading, gradients and the many stripes with large solid flat shapes. Use at most three broad taupe marking shapes total. Use a uniform thick warm dark brown outline with smooth rounded curves. Simplify paws into a single tucked shape, omit individual toes and whiskers. Keep a single curved stroke for the closed eye. Make the tail one broad clean inward curl. The result should look like a deliberate minimal vector brand symbol that reads at 48 pixels, not a detailed illustration. One centered logo only, square canvas with generous margins, genuinely transparent background, no lettering, no decorative elements, no presentation board.
